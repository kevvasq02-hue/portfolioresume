About

I work across disciplines because every idea asks for a different form.

I'm Kevin Vasquez, a Communications student at the College of Staten Island studying Digital Design and Marketing. My practice moves between drawing, motion, fashion, and visual storytelling.

I'm interested in images that hold personality and memory. Whether I am building an animated scene, studying a face, or modeling clothing, I look for the details that make a story feel lived-in.
